Field Note — On Measurement

Competency as a vector, not a verdict

The percentages above are best read as living, dynamic vectors rather than static benchmarks. A proficiency score reflects applied fluency at a specific point in time—accelerating through deep experimentation, and designed to evolve as we transition into upcoming academic and research milestones like Deep Learning and AI Agents.

None of these figures are arbitrary. They represent a cumulative synthesis where AI has acted as an immense accelerator—drastically compressing the learning curve for complex domains like Gradient Boosting, while remaining anchored by rigorous foundational study (such as dedicated EDA coursework). Conversely, auxiliary execution tools like Linux environments or Pytest reflect pragmatic, AI-assisted leverage where capability is unlocked dynamically, proving that modern engineering is defined by knowing how to wield intelligence to bridge gaps and scale capabilities toward full mastery.
